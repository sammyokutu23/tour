# ADR 0006 — Honest content: illustrations, demo flags and no fabricated trust signals

**Status:** accepted · 2026-10-21

**Decision.**
- Seed real Kenyan destinations with conservative descriptions.
- Seed only fictional providers and listings, flagged `is_demo` and badged in the UI.
- Seed no reviews or ratings, and mark no demo provider as verified.
- Use original brand-coloured SVG landscapes instead of unlicensed photography; licensed media can be attached later with credit and licence fields.
- Label travel times as estimates and never draw straight lines as routes.

**Consequences.** The product can be demonstrated without misleading claims. Launch requires onboarding and verifying real providers, and optionally licensing photography.
